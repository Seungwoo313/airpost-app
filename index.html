What is Airpost?

An app for writing to pen pals around the world. Every letter boards a plane, and arrives when it lands.

How long does a letter take?

It depends on the distance between your airports and the plane you choose. A neighbor city takes minutes. The other side of the world takes about half a day.

Is it safe to write to strangers?

You can block anyone and report any letter from inside the app. Airpost is for people 18 and older.

Can my pen pal see where I live?

They see your home airport, nothing more. Airpost never stores your exact coordinates.
